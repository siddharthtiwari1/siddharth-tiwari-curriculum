## Topics

- Cost of a wrong decision: false alarms (false positives) and misses (false negatives)
- Where AI is not validated (never tested for this situation)
- Escalation path: who a case goes to when the AI is unsure
- Autonomy matrix: a grid of what AI decides alone, what needs approval and what stays human
- Hands-on: Tally the false alarms and misses in a supplied month of one workflow's automated decisions in a sheet, then fill an autonomy matrix for one of your own workflows: what AI decides alone, what needs approval, what stays human, using the supplied severity and reversibility rule

## Learning objectives

- Describe the business consequence of a false positive and a false negative
- Analyze a deployment context a model was never validated for
- Design an escalation path for automated decisions the model is uncertain about
- Classify each step of a workflow as AI-alone, AI-with-approval or human-only by severity and reversibility
