## Topics

- What an AI integration costs in year one: licences, usage, build, people
- Hidden costs: data cleaning, change management, review time, monitoring
- Token prices in practice: the cost of one request (an API call) to your own assistant
- Cost per task: a person, an assistant, an agent
- ROI (return on investment) and payback period
- Go, pilot or stop
- Hands-on: Work out the cost of one request to your assistant or agent from its token prices, then build the first-year cost, ROI and payback sheet for it and decide go, pilot or stop against the supplied hurdle ROI and risk rating

## Learning objectives

- Compute the first-year cost of an AI integration from licence, usage, build and people costs
- Identify the hidden costs AI projects miss: data cleaning, change management, human review time and ongoing monitoring
- Compute the cost of a single API call from input and output token prices
- Compute the cost per task of a person, an AI assistant and an agent from supplied figures
- Compute the return on investment and payback period of an AI use case from stated costs and benefits
- Recommend go, pilot or stop for an AI use case, justifying by its return on investment and its risk
