## Topics

- Vibe coding: writing an app specification for AI to build
- Building and iterating with follow-up prompts when a screen does not match the spec
- Showing your published assistant inside the app's front end (the screens people see) by embedding it (placing it inside the page)
- Testing what the AI built: click through every screen, try bad inputs, check no key or password shows on any page, and see where your data goes
- Hands-on: Describe a small request-form app, build it with an AI app builder, embed your published Dify assistant in it and test one full request and reply

## Learning objectives

- Build a working application from a natural-language specification with an AI app builder or coding assistant
- Diagnose a generated no-code screen or workflow step that does not match the stated requirement
- Configure a pre-built integration, such as email, payment or a published AI assistant, inside a no-code application
- Test an AI-built app for broken flows, missing input checks and keys left inside the app
