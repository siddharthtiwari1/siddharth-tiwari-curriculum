## Topics

- Four kinds of risk: credit, market, operational and compliance
- Classifying an AI risk flag
- From risk score to approve, review or decline
- Cost of a missed risk versus a false alarm
- Hands-on: Classify 20 supplied flagged cases with AI and work out the cost of the false alarms in a sheet

## Learning objectives

- Classify an AI-assisted risk flag as credit, market, operational or compliance risk from a stated scenario
- Describe how a decision support system turns a model output into an operating decision
- Describe the business consequence of a false positive and a false negative
- Compute the expected cost of a classifier from a stated per-error cost matrix
