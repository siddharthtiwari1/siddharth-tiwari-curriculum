## Topics

- Use-case map by function: marketing and finance
- Estimating business impact from volume and cost assumptions
- Custom build or vendor: the break-even volume
- Comparing two vendor case-study solutions
- Hands-on: Map the AI use cases from this module by function, then quantify one, such as AI-personalised offers at a quick-commerce company like Blinkit, and compare two vendor case-study solutions for it in a sheet

## Learning objectives

- Construct a map of AI use cases by business function, naming the value each could create
- Compute the annual value of a proposed solution from stated assumptions
- Compute the break-even volume at which per-use savings or costs match a one-time cost
- Evaluate a GenAI vendor proposal against a stated set of enterprise requirements, scoring each requirement met or gapped
