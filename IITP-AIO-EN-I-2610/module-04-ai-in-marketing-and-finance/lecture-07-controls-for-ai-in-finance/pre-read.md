## Topics

- Audit logging: a record of who or what decided, when and on what data
- Sign-off checklist: inputs, assumptions and source trace
- Tasks needing a named approver
- Tracing each figure to its source
- Hands-on: Build a sign-off checklist and an audit log for an AI-drafted forecast, using the weekly sales forecast a quick-commerce company such as Zepto sends its leadership team (supplied figures)

## Learning objectives

- State what must be logged for an automated decision to be auditable
- Design a sign-off checklist for an AI-assisted financial forecast, naming who reviews it and what they check
- Evaluate which automated decisions need a human reviewer, justifying by severity and reversibility
- Validate every claim or assumption in a written analysis, draft or forecast against its source facts or figures
