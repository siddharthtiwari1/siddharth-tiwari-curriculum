## Topics

- Conversion (the share of people who buy) and cost per customer
- Budget priorities
- Next best offer: AI picks which offer to send to which segment
- Testing a campaign change
- Hands-on: Split a supplied budget across channels, ask AI for its recommendation and compare it with last period's return

## Learning objectives

- Compute the conversion rate and cost per customer for each channel from stated campaign results
- Prioritize a stated marketing budget across channels using an AI-generated recommendation, justified against last period's return
- Describe how a recommendation system uses past behaviour to rank what to show next
- Describe how a business compares a campaign change with an unchanged group to see its effect
