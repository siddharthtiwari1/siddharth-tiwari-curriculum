## Topics

- Lead with the recommendation
- Headline slide and backup slides
- Defending one number under challenge

- Hands-on: Present a supplied AI business case in three slides and defend one number against a challenge from the class

## Learning objectives

- Construct a talk opening that states the recommendation before the method
- Construct backup slides holding the detail removed from the main deck
- Analyze which assumption a business case is most sensitive to
- Compute the effect on a top-line metric of a stated change in one driver
