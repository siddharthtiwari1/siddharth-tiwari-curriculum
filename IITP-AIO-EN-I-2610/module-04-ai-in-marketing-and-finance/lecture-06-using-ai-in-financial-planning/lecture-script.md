## Topics

- Where AI helps finance: planning (revenue and cost forecasts), treasury (managing the company's cash) and reporting (AI-drafted management reports)
- Checking assumptions
- Rebuilding in a spreadsheet
- What moves the forecast
- Hands-on: Ask AI for a 13-week cash-flow forecast from supplied receipts and payments, rebuild it by hand in a sheet and explain every difference

## Learning objectives

- Describe where AI assists financial planning, treasury and management reporting
- Validate every claim or assumption in a written analysis, draft or forecast against its source facts or figures
- Analyze the differences between an AI-drafted forecast and a hand-built spreadsheet forecast on the same inputs
- Compute the effect on a top-line metric of a stated change in one driver
