## Topics

- Content licences
- Personalisation guideline
- Writing on-brand prompts
- Judging generated images against a creative brief
- Brand consistency check
- Hands-on: Write a one-page brand guide (voice, words to avoid, visual rules), then produce copy, an image and a voiceover for two customer segments and check them against it

## Learning objectives

- Classify commercial uses of generated content by the licence term that governs each
- Write a content-personalisation guideline naming what AI may vary per segment and what stays fixed
- Build an AI-drafted business document from a stated brief, covering its content and tone requirements
- Evaluate generated images against a written creative brief
- Evaluate an AI-drafted message or document against a stated standard before it is used
