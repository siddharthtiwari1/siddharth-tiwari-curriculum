## Topics

- How AI suggests a price from demand and competitor prices
- Which driver moves the price most (what the price is most sensitive to)
- Price guardrails
- Recommendations: judging what a recommender shows
- Hands-on: Use a scenario table to see which driver moves a price most, set price guardrails, then judge a supplied recommendation list for one customer

## Learning objectives

- Describe how an AI pricing tool uses demand and competitor prices to suggest a price
- Analyze which assumption a business case is most sensitive to
- Configure a rule that keeps an AI price recommendation within a stated acceptable range
- Evaluate a recommendation list for one customer against their purchase history, naming one useful and one poor suggestion
