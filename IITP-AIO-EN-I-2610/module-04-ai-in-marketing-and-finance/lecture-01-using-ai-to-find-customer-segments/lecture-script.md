## Topics

- AI segment profiles
- Using a segment
- Customer journey map: where each segment drops off
- Fairness check (no group unfairly left out or targeted) and relevance check
- Comparing segments
- Hands-on: Segment a supplied 500-row customer file with an AI data-analysis tool, name three segments, then, from the supplied funnel counts, map one segment's journey from first visit to purchase and mark its biggest drop-offs

## Learning objectives

- Build an AI-assisted customer segment profile from stated behavioural and demographic signals
- Describe how a business uses a customer segment to choose a message, an offer or a channel
- Build a customer journey map for one segment from supplied funnel data, naming where customers drop off
- Evaluate an AI-proposed targeting segment against a stated fairness and relevance check
- Select which of two customer segments to target first, justifying by size, value and behaviour
