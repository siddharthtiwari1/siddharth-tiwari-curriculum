## Topics

- Competitor comparison from public sources
- Themes in customer reviews
- Checking AI review summaries
- Findings that surprise
- Hands-on: Build a competitor table for one product with a source for each row, then group 200 supplied customer reviews, such as PhonePe app reviews or Google Maps reviews of a store, into themes with AI and check the summary against a sample read of the originals, then write one finding that would surprise the product team

## Learning objectives

- Construct an AI-generated output that attaches a supporting source to each claim
- Cluster a supplied set of support tickets into named groups with an AI tool
- Classify a generated claim as grounded in or unsupported by the provided context
- Write a finding that contradicts the audience's stated expectation
