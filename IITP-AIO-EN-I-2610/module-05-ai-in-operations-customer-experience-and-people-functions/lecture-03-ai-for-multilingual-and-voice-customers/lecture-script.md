## Topics

- Two ways voice AI works: speech straight in (audio-native), or speech to text first (a two-step pipeline), compared on speed (latency) and accuracy (fidelity)
- Multilingual grounded answers
- Voice assistant
- Why transcripts go wrong
- Hands-on: Build a Gemini Gem (a saved custom assistant) grounded on a supplied support FAQ, ask it a customer question by voice in Hindi or another Indian language, check where the transcript went wrong, then ask the same question in Gemini Live and compare speed and accuracy

## Learning objectives

- Compare an audio-native model with a transcribe-then-generate pipeline on latency and fidelity
- Build a grounded multilingual service-assistant response from a customer query and the company's own support documents
- Build a voice assistant that transcribes a customer's spoken question accurately before answering it
- Diagnose transcription errors caused by audio quality, accent or domain vocabulary
