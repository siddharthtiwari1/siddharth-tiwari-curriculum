## Topics

- Demand forecasting, including seasonality (patterns that repeat each year)
- Inventory recommendations under a disruption
- Disruption risk: low, medium or high
- How the forecast feeds production planning and inventory
- Hands-on: Forecast demand from 24 months of supplied sales with AI, then test the forecast against a disruption scenario

## Learning objectives

- Build an AI-assisted demand forecast from historical sales data and a stated seasonality pattern
- Evaluate an AI inventory recommendation against a stated disruption scenario before acting on it
- Classify a supply-chain disruption risk as low, medium or high from an AI-generated resilience assessment
- Describe how a demand forecast feeds inventory and production planning decisions
