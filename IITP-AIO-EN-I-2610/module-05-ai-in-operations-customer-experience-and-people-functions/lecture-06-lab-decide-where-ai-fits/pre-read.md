## Topics

- Who a product could harm
- Workflow map
- Which steps to automate first
- Which steps stay human
- Hands-on: Map one workflow from your own function on a slide and mark where AI assists and where a person stays in charge, using the supplied severity and reversibility rule

## Learning objectives

- Predict the groups a proposed AI product could harm before it is built
- Classify each step of a workflow as AI-alone, AI-with-approval or human-only by severity and reversibility
- Prioritize the steps of a manual process for automation, justifying by frequency and error rate
- Evaluate which automated decisions need a human reviewer, justifying by severity and reversibility
