## Topics

- The hiring funnel (stages from sourcing to shortlist) with AI
- Bias (unfair results for one group) that feeds itself
- HR policy assistant
- Questions before AI shortlisting
- Hands-on: Shortlist six supplied sample resumes with AI against a job description, answer one employee question from a supplied HR policy, and list the questions to ask before using the shortlist

## Learning objectives

- Describe where AI assists a hiring funnel from sourcing to shortlisting
- Describe a feedback loop that amplifies a model's bias after deployment
- Write a prompt that instructs the model to answer only from supplied reference text
- Identify the questions a leader should ask before using an AI tool to shortlist candidates
