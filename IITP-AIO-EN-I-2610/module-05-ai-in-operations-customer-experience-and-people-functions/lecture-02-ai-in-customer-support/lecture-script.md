## Topics

- Support automation and its deflection rate (the share of questions AI answers without a person)
- The economics: cost per contact for a person and for the bot, and the monthly saving
- Satisfaction floor: the lowest customer score you will accept
- Why deflection can mislead: a stand-in measure (proxy) can drift from the real goal
- When to hand over to a human
- Hands-on: Paste a supplied FAQ into a chat, answer 20 sample customer questions from it, work out the deflection rate and the monthly saving from supplied costs per contact, and set the hand-over rule

## Learning objectives

- Compute the annual value of a proposed solution from stated assumptions
- Evaluate an AI support-deflection case against a stated customer-satisfaction floor before scaling it
- Explain why a proxy measure can diverge from the outcome it stands for
- Evaluate which automated decisions need a human reviewer, justifying by severity and reversibility
