## Topics

- The supplied AI system and a supplied governance checklist covering bias, privacy, regulation and the model card
- Logging every gap against the DPDP Act and the EU AI Act
- Prioritising the three biggest launch risks
- Ship or no-ship against the supplied severity scale and ship threshold
- Hands-on: Review a supplied AI system against the checklist, log the gaps and recommend ship or no-ship

## Learning objectives

- Evaluate a proposed deployment against a stated responsible-AI checklist
- Evaluate a consent notice against the DPDP Act's requirements for a stated processing purpose
- List the obligations a high-risk AI system must satisfy under the EU AI Act
- Prioritize the three biggest launch risks a sample AI system poses, justifying by harm and reversibility
- Recommend a ship or no-ship decision for a sample AI system, justifying by its logged compliance gaps
