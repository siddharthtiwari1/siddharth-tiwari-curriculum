## Topics

- Types of bias
- How data builds in bias
- Checking a shortlist by group
- Testing an AI for stereotypes
- Hands-on: Check a supplied AI shortlist by group and test a chatbot for stereotyped answers

## Learning objectives

- Classify a stated bias as historical, sampling, labelling or deployment bias
- Analyze how a data-collection process encodes historical bias against a group
- Analyze an AI hiring tool's shortlist by candidate subgroup to check for a fairness gap
- Test an LLM prompt for stereotyped output across substituted demographic terms
