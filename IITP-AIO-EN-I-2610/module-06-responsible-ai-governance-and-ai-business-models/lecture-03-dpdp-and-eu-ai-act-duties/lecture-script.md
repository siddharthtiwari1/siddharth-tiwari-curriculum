## Topics

- India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act: consent and legitimate uses
- Rights of the data principal (the person the data is about)
- Data minimisation (collect only what you need) and retention (how long you keep it)
- Checking a consent notice
- EU AI Act risk tiers
- What high-risk systems must do
- Hands-on: Check a sample consent notice against a DPDP checklist and place a sample AI system in its EU AI Act tier

## Learning objectives

- State the lawful basis and consent required for a stated use of personal data
- List the rights a data principal holds under the DPDP Act
- Describe the data minimisation and retention obligations on a stated pipeline
- Evaluate a consent notice against the DPDP Act's requirements for a stated processing purpose
- Classify an AI use case by the risk tier a stated regulation assigns it
- List the obligations a high-risk AI system must satisfy under the EU AI Act
