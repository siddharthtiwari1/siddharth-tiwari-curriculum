## Topics

- Established and emerging AI business models: who pays and for what
- Pricing an AI product: usage-based (pay per use), subscription, outcome-based (pay per result) or embedded (inside another product's price)
- Recommending a model for your industry: willingness to pay and switching cost (the lock-in from 10.1)
- Agentic ecosystems: agents from different vendors working together (the A2A standard)
- Where regulation falls behind
- Judging claims about AI's societal impact
- Hands-on: Place three real AI companies' pricing on a comparison table, including Microsoft Copilot's move from per-seat licences (a fee per user) towards usage-based pricing for agents, compare an established and an emerging model against the supplied adoption barriers, and recommend a model for your industry

## Learning objectives

- Compare an established AI business model with an emerging one for a stated industry's adoption barriers
- Classify an AI product's business model as usage-based, subscription, outcome-based or embedded
- Recommend an AI business model for a stated industry, justifying by willingness to pay and switching cost
- Describe how two independent agents exchange a task and its result over an agent-to-agent protocol
- Predict the regulatory gap a stated agentic AI deployment is likely to expose
- Validate every claim or assumption in a written analysis, draft or forecast against its source facts or figures
