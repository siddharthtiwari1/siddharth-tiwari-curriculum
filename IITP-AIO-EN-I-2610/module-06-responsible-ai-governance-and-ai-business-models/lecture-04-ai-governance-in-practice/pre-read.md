## Topics

- Responsible AI principles a policy rests on: privacy, security, compliance and ethics
- A named owner, reviewer, review cadence and escalation contact for each AI system
- The model card: intended use, data, limits
- Incident response when AI output harms a customer
- A one-page governance policy
- Hands-on: Write a short model card and a one-page AI governance policy for one AI system in your organisation, naming the principle each rule protects

## Learning objectives

- Explain the responsible AI principles covering privacy, security, compliance and ethics, with a workplace example of each
- Write a one-page AI governance policy naming the owner, the reviewer, the review cadence and the escalation contact
- Write a model card documenting intended use, training data, metrics and limitations
- Describe the response steps when an AI output harms a customer: contain, inform, correct and review
