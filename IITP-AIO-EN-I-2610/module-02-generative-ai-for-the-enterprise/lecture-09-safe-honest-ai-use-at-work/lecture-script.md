## Topics

- Grounded versus unsupported claims
- Hidden instructions in documents (prompt injection)
- When to disclose AI use
- An acceptable-use policy for your team
- Hands-on: Try a document with hidden instructions on two chatbots, then, for a Tier-2 city hospital chain, list the patient data that must never go into a public chatbot and write the team's acceptable-use policy, including the paste rule from Module 1

## Learning objectives

- Classify a generated claim as grounded in or unsupported by the provided context
- Describe prompt injection and jailbreaking as attack classes on an LLM application
- State when the use of AI assistance must be disclosed to a reader, client or assessor
- Design an acceptable-use policy for generative AI tools in a workplace
