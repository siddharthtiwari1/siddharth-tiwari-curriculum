## Topics

- Forecasting monthly spend: the price per token in and out, times the number of requests
- What happens when you hit a usage limit (a quota or rate limit)
- Comparing platforms on cost, latency, data residency (which country stores your data) and lock-in (how hard it is to leave)
- Buy, build or use an open model: cost, control, time to value
- Scoring a vendor proposal
- AI vendor contracts: clauses to check
- Hands-on: Forecast monthly spend for a supplied usage profile from a supplied price table taken from the providers' price pages, then score two supplied vendor proposals on a scorecard and mark the risky clauses in a sample contract

## Learning objectives

- Forecast monthly spend for an LLM feature at a stated request volume
- Describe the constraints of a no-code or GenAI platform, including quotas, rate limits and lock-in
- Evaluate a GenAI vendor proposal against a stated set of enterprise requirements, scoring each requirement met or gapped
- Compare buying a vendor tool, building in-house and using an open model on cost, control and time to value
- Identify the clauses to check in an AI vendor contract covering data use, exit, liability and support
