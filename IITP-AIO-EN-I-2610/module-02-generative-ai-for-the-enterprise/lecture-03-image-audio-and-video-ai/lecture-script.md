## Topics

- How image, audio and video models differ from text models
- Writing an image prompt: subject, style, composition, lighting
- Common image failures: garbled text, hands, object counts
- Voiceovers from a script with text-to-speech
- What video models can and cannot do yet
- Consent before using a real person's face or voice
- Hands-on: Generate an image from a creative brief and produce a voiceover for a 30-second script, the instructor demonstrates a video model

## Learning objectives

- Describe what distinguishes a multimodal model from a text-only model
- Write a text-to-image prompt specifying subject, style, composition and lighting
- Identify common image-generation failure modes such as rendered text, hands and object counts
- Build a voiceover from a written script with a text-to-speech tool
- Describe what current AI video tools can and cannot do for a business
- Identify the consent needed before a real person's face or voice is used in AI-generated media
