## Topics

- What open-source and open-weight models are: models whose files anyone can download and run
- Where your data goes with a hosted AI service (run by the provider), and where it stays with a model you run
- Three ways to run an open model: a laptop, company servers, a private cloud
- Open or hosted: cost, control and quality
- Choosing for a stated task, using public leaderboards for answer quality and speed (latency: how long an answer takes)
- Which documents may leave the company
- Hands-on: Ask an open model (Gemma) and a hosted chatbot the same work question and compare, see where both sit on LMArena and Artificial Analysis for quality and speed (instructor shows), watch the instructor run an open model with no internet, then sort five document types from your own work by where each may be sent

## Learning objectives

- Compare open-weight and proprietary hosted models on cost, control and deployment
- Describe the three ways to run an open model: on a laptop, on company servers or on a private cloud
- Recommend an open model or a hosted AI service for a stated task, justifying by data sensitivity, cost and quality
- Identify information unsafe to paste into a public AI chatbot
